Fix, unchanged from my 2026-11-21 note: retry `ConnectionRefused` until the deadline in `connect`, `connect_v1` and the raw connect near `:320`, as `HolderHub::open` already does. My probe then passed the cases that needed a retry. It is test-only; I can open it as one PR if you give me a slot.
