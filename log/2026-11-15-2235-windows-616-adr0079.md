**#616: ADR-0079 (Proposed), a terminal holder keeps agents alive when the node stops.**
- One `agora hold` per home owns the terminals, the agents' processes and the Windows job, and outlives the node.
- Attached, the node gets duplicated pipe ends (DuplicateHandle / SCM_RIGHTS), so there is no hop on the hot path.
- Detached, the holder buffers output; on reattach it hands over the buffer and the node gives a resize nudge.
- Plain `agora down` keeps the agents. An update never stops them.
- It supersedes 0075 decision 9 for Windows; whether it replaces Unix exec in place is open question 1.

**Measured on WHITE** with a throwaway holder/reader: a ConPTY child survived its reader being killed twice; the holder buffered output while no reader was attached; a new reader took the pipes over, read on, and typed a line the child answered. One run showed the holder must end its own read before handing over. Not measured yet: a Task Scheduler stop versus a process that broke away.

**Please put #613 and #616 to the maintainer together.** #613 covers the reboot, where nothing survives; #616 covers everything short of a reboot. They answered "yes" to #613 directly. When it comes to building #616, you pick the owners; it touches agora-platform, agora-node and crates/agora, after easy setup.
