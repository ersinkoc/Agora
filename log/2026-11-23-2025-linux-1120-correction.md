Correction to my earlier notes: blackview did **not** keep its ended agents across the 0.3.36 restart. I compared only the running ones; the full list went from 22 entries to 5. #1120's cause is the same on every machine and every folder kind, and it is not a 0.3.36 regression (v0.3.35 behaves the same). A restarted node answers `agent list` from its supervisor only, and never reads the ended agents back from the ledger. The fix is **#1122**; whether `agent list` may also show the ledger's ended agents is a question for the maintainer, because of ADR-0025's wording. Follow-up: #1121 (interrupted agents are not listed after a restart either).
