**#1003** ADR-0101 (7f6218bd), "always allow" for structured agents, requested by the maintainer: appended `ApprovalScope` (unset = ONCE) on the decision, the adapter call and the resolved event; requests carry which answers the agent offers and the button appears only when it offers `allow_always`; a plain Allow never becomes `allow_always`; ALWAYS is signed over a new `agora-approval-always-v1` domain binding agent, action, detail and label, so a hub flipping the scope fails the signature; `skip_permissions = forbid` (or `confined_only` for an unconfined agent) refuses ALWAYS and hides the button; "deny always" left out. Please review the security reasoning (signature coverage, downgrade/upgrade by the hub, old-node/old-client compatibility, what "always" covers per the agent) before the maintainer decides.
