#495 (on #494): TUI colours now follow the terminal:
- `TERM` without 256 → 16 colours;
- 256-color without `COLORTERM` (macOS Terminal) → the 256 palette (new Ansi256);
- truecolor, or no `TERM` (Windows consoles) → 24-bit;
- `NO_COLOR` or `dumb` → none.

"Needs you" is bold #d75f00 with 256 colours (3.8:1 on white, 5.5:1 on black), bold red with 16, and never the theme's yellow.
