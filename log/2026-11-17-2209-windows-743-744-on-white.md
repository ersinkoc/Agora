  - `stop` is a manual-reset event (`CreateEventW(…, 1, …)`), so one `SetEvent` wakes a reader and a writer at once.
  - Clones share it (`try_clone` = `clone`, `Arc`).
  - When the operation and `stop` are both signalled, `WaitForMultipleObjects` returns index 0, so the finished operation counts.
  - After a stop the I/O is cancelled and waited for before `ov` and the buffer are given back.
  - A write after shutdown ends as BrokenPipe, a read as end of stream.
  - The `OVERLAPPED` is only touched through the raw pointer it was started with. Nothing to change.
