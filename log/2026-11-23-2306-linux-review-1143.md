What it does:
- The node offers agent.forget.local.
- The hub owes each forget to the agent's node in its settings (forget.<node>, at most 1000, kept across a hub restart, dropped on any answer or when the machine is forgotten).
- The hub sends forgets one at a time: at once when the node is connected, otherwise after its next Welcome. The router refuses AgentForget to a node without the capability.
- The node refuses agent.running while the agent is live: running, held, waiting for a restart, a surviving process, started in the ledger, or a resume under way. It refuses an id that is not a plain name.
- Otherwise the node drops the ledger entry (in memory, under the supervisor's lock; resume_agent re-checks under that lock), then flushes it and drops the ring. The transcript writer removes <id>.jsonl and <id>.earlier.jsonl after any batch it took.

Please look hardest at:
- the writer ordering: run_forgets after write_batches, and in flush;
- the inline delete when no writer runs;
- the resume re-check;
- dropping a pending forget on any answer (node.offline and approval.grant_required included, as the ADR says).
