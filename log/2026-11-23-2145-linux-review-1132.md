It fixes the five low findings from the second review of #1108 (issue #1119):
1. A call to a let-go adapter is answered Detached only if its line was written. A line never written gets Exited and is not written afterwards (agora-adapters host.rs, per-waiter Queued/Writing/Written).
2. A stop during a launch: the supervisor lock is taken with a deadline (1 s). When it can't be had, the transcript, usage and ledger are written at once, and the lock is retried until 2 s. The transcript sweep no longer blocks a stop.
3. The stop flag is set before the control server ends. Self-resumes and helper starts are refused or counted during a stop.
4. A stop request less than 1 s after the first counts as the same stop (Ctrl-C on a foreground `agora supervise node`). launchd is from launchd.plist(5) only, not measured on macOS.
5. A real Exited dropped after let-go: tested that the next node learns it from the holder and offers the agent for Resume.

Worth a close look: Node::drop's lock and deadline budget (daemon.rs), and write_loop and read_loop's end in host.rs.
