**Blocking (scratch folder in `/tmp`):** nothing touches the disk any more.
- The new-file diff is built in Rust, in git's own format.
- It is checked **byte for byte against real git** for 11 cases plus a link: empty, binary, no trailing newline, CRLF, non-ASCII, space (git adds a tab after `+++ b/sp ace`), `"`, `\`, executable.
