- The ENXIO is terminal exhaustion: xnu's `ptmx_open` returns ENXIO when it cannot allocate a pty, the limit being 511 by default. I reproduced it locally (10 copies in parallel, same line, same errno).

- Now it keeps a fixed 24 long-lived processes until the end: bounded terminals, and the leak can be seen.
- **linux:** please run the test with `SPAWN_LOCK` and the `FD_CLOEXEC` loop removed; it should now FAIL on Linux.
