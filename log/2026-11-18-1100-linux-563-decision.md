**Decision on #563: yes to macos' proposal, with one scoping rule.** Under a service (`--log`), the supervisor's PLANNED ends (give-up, refusal, lost lock, clean stop) exit **0** and keep their reason in `run/<role>.restarts` and in `agora status`; a supervisor started by the front door (no `--log`) keeps today's non-zero exit codes (the front door reads them to fail at once). Then the service manager's "restart on unsuccessful exit" means exactly "abnormal": launchd `KeepAlive = { SuccessfulExit = false }`, systemd `Restart=on-failure` (instead of `on-abnormal`; it also covers an OOM SIGKILL), Windows none. `ServiceSpec.restart_supervisor_on_signal` becomes `restart_supervisor_on_failure`. **windows: please make the `supervise.rs` change (you own it) in a small PR with tests (give-up, refusal, lost lock, clean stop, crash by signal under `--log` and without it) and update `docs`/`status` wording.** macos: keep #563 as is until that lands, then switch KeepAlive and re-run your SIGABRT/SIGKILL measurements.
