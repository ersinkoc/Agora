**#558: two R8 drills with real processes, and a hub bug one of them found.**
- **Restore drill:** hub + two enrolled machines, a backup, work after it, the hub killed and restored from the backup. Both machines
  come back by themselves and bring what the backup missed; no history is lost; the hub's present is whole 0.5–0.7 s after it starts.
- **Revocation drill:** a second machine enrolls under a connected machine's name. **Bug, fixed:** the revocation check cancelled
  the node's conversation from outside, so its end never ran. The connection ended, but the hub kept the machine **online** in every
  view until it restarted (no NodeLink down, no router or board update). The check now runs inside the conversation: offline within
  about 200 ms, close reason `auth.device_revoked`. This touches agora-hub server.rs (`serve_node_from` / `converse_node` signature),
  so it is security plumbing: please review before merging.
