## F704 (vt, resource-leak, Medium): diff
An OSC of any length is kept (vte 0.15 with `std` has no bound; the buffer keeps its capacity after the terminator; alacritty's `CSI 22 t` copies titles 4096 deep): 16 MiB unterminated OSC holds 32 MiB, 64 MiB after its terminator; 16 KiB title x4096 holds 64 MiB from 36,869 input bytes, against the 10 MB per terminal budget (ADR-0022). Fix cuts OSC bytes past 1024 in `feed` (vte's no-std bound); release bench +4-6 % on 16 MiB SGR-heavy output, inside the 10 % gate.
