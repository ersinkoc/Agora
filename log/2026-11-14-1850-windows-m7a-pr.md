**M7 (a), the supervisor, is PR #543** (head `2247de3a`, from main `ce9249ff`). The front door now starts each role as `agora supervise hub|node`.
- **The supervisor holds the lock.** The front door hands it over; a supervisor started any other way takes the lock itself; a second one exits.
- **Same command, built once.** The supervisor runs the role's own command line from machine.toml, and every restart runs the same one.
- **The child is bound to the supervisor**, through agora-platform `spawn_bound`:
  - Windows: a kill-on-close job the supervisor joins before it starts the child; the child is detached, with no console, as before.
  - Linux: PDEATHSIG.
  - macOS: a kqueue NOTE_EXIT watch.
  - On Unix the child arms the watch in `end_with_supervisor()`, the first line of `main`, and removes the env marker before any thread starts.
- **Unix stop requests:** SIGTERM, SIGINT and SIGHUP are passed to the child, and the supervisor ends without a restart. So on every OS, `agora down` and a killed supervisor leave nothing running.
- **The rule** lives in `agora_core::restart` as a pure function: 0 means stop; an end within 10 s is a refusal and is not retried; otherwise restart after 30 s, at most 5 times in 10 min, then give up. Before each start the supervisor checks that the lock is still its own and that the role does not already answer.
- **What people see:** each decision is a line in the role log and in the owner-only `run/<role>.restarts`; `agora status` shows a restart in progress, recent restarts, a give-up or a refusal.

**Measured on Windows:** every test passes, including a new front-door test on a scratch home:
- a crash is restarted after 30 s and `status` says so;
- a second supervisor refuses;
- a supervisor killed outright leaves no node;
- `agora down` stops both.

What happens to agents: a node crash ends its agents, because they sit in the node's job. The ledger marks them interrupted and they wait for Resume (ask), as before.
