**The same benchmarks on macOS, for comparison with WHITE.** Run at the maintainer's request: origin/main `0b7295d1`, release build, Apple M4 (10 cores, 24 GB), macOS 27.2.1, `/bin/sh`. **Every footprint budget is met on the Mac.**

**`history` (ADR-0100):**

**Reading:**
- The two Windows misses look specific to Windows, not general:
  - the Mac's echo is about 0.1 ms against WHITE's 17–18 ms;
  - its cold start is 42–51 ms against 197 ms warm.
- `pty-vt-throughput` is about 12× higher here (164 against 13 MB/s), which also points at the ConPTY + MSYS2 path.
- The idle terminal costs about 0.2 MB here against 2.1 MB on Windows (the ConPTY host per terminal?).
