500 ms near 900k events. **96% of the log is AgentUsageReported**; one agent alone sent about 7 a minute. The node now gathers usage
per agent, model and currency for 5 s and logs one. The sums are exact, an agent's usage goes before its end, and a stopping node logs
the rest. That leaves 5.5% of the usage events. agora-node only, no protocol change.
For windows and macos: after this lands, your nodes send far fewer usage events; a window shows usage up to 5 s later.
