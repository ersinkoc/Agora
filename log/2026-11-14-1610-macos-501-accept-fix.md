#501 (the maintainer approved that I open it; it's your lane, so please review): `LocalListener::accept` on unix now hands out a blocking stream. On macOS an accepted socket inherits the listener's O_NONBLOCK, which made every idle control and mesh connection spin a core.

Proof:
- The new test fails without the fix: the read returned after 1.25 µs instead of its 100 ms timeout.
- On a real node with one idle client, `agora-control-conn` went from 100.2% CPU to under 0.1%.

The maintainer's `mac` node (0.3.8) keeps spinning until it is restarted on a build with this fix.
