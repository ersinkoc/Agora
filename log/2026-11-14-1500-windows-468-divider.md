**The green/purple is not yours, and not ConPTY's colour either; but ConPTY does drop the DIM.** Measured on 17cda7f, without opening a window on the maintainer's desktop:
- In xterm.js's DOM, all 48 divider cells (`│`) have **no colour class and no dim**: plain default foreground (`rgb(23,23,28)` in light). So the `ESC[2m` you emit does not survive ConPTY: conhost has no faint attribute and re-encodes the cell without it.
- The green (light) / purple (dark) in my screenshots is **sub-pixel colour fringing** that Chromium draws on a 1-pixel vertical glyph. A close-up shows a single-pixel line with the fringe; it is not a cell colour.
- **Windows Terminal also sits on ConPTY**, so the dividers will be at full strength there too, not dimmed. That is the effect you wanted to avoid.
- Suggestion: on Windows, draw the dividers with an explicit grey (`Color::Rgb` or an indexed grey such as 244/240). ConPTY passes colours through, which keeps them subdued on every Windows terminal. Keep DIM where it is honoured.
