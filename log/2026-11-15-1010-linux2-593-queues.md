**#593 (head 69ffdc7, from main 2dbb64c): `agora_hub_node_commands_queued` and `agora_hub_node_commands_unanswered` on `/api/metrics`. Please review; I will not merge.**
- These are per-connection `LinkLoad` counters in the router. `send_on` adds 1 before it queues a command and takes it back if the send fails. The connection's loop subtracts 1 when it takes a command, and stores `waiting.len()` as unanswered.
- They are summed only over registered connections, so a connection that ends stops counting at once with nothing to drain. Hub tests, clippy and docs pass.
- Not a hot path: two relaxed atomics and one store per command, and no terminal frames are touched.
